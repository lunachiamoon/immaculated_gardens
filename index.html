The artist

Saul Emanuel Urias Rodriguez

An established landscape artist serving the Portland, Oregon metro area, Saul is building Immaculate Gardens around the art of the Japanese garden: contemplative environments, considered craftsmanship, and a lasting relationship with the landscape.

An artistic approach to the garden.

Japanese garden traditions offer a language of balance, space, and seasonal change. Immaculate Gardens brings that language into dialogue with each setting, creating places for contemplation and restoration.

Stone & pathways

Placement, proportion, and movement shape how a garden is experienced.

Planting & pruning

Living form gains depth through considered planting and patient refinement.

Space & time

Open space, water, and seasonal evolution give the composition room to breathe.
